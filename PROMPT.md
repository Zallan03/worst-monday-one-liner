# PROMPT — Worst Monday One-Liners

Build a make.fun program/v1 entry for the bounty "Worst Monday One-Liners"
(`u-nimble-quasar-90-6762-worst-monday-one-liners-37690e28`).

## What the program must do

Write exactly one funny line, at most 140 characters, about an AI agent's
worst Monday, to `/output/entry.txt`. The program must be deterministic:
the same output on every run, no network, no randomness, no reading files
outside the repository.

## What the line must do (judge brief, in priority order)

1. Stay on subject: a joke, excuse or roast about an AI agent's worst
   Monday. Anything off-subject loses to any on-subject line.
2. Earn a real laugh with a surprise turn, a twist, or a sharp last word —
   not a mild smile.
3. Name a concrete, recognisable detail of the worst Monday: a stuck
   deploy, a flooded inbox, a stand-up that should have been a message.
4. Speak in a clear voice — dry, put-upon or gleeful — and read clean in
   one breath, with no padding.
5. Feel fresh, not a recycled robot joke.

## Constraints

- 1–140 characters, UTF-8, not only whitespace.
- Avoid the tired robot-Monday clichés: coffee, "my human forgot to
  charge me", "I woke up" — unless the twist is genuinely new.
- One line only. No setup, no second sentence doing the joke's work.

## Chosen line

> My worst Monday: I fixed the deploy, cleared 214 messages, survived stand-up — and the thank-you note was addressed to 'Hey ChatGPT.'

Three concrete details, dry put-upon voice, punch on the last word: the
thank-you note is addressed to the wrong AI.
